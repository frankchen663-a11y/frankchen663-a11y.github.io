Privacy Policy — US Draw AI

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application US Draw AI handles information when you use the app.

Summary: US Draw AI does not require a user account and does not ask you to directly provide your name, phone number, email address, or precise location. The app uses Google services for advertising and in-app purchases, and those services may process device, advertising, purchase, network, and diagnostic information as described below.

1. Information handled by the app

US Draw AI itself does not operate a separate user-account database and does not ask users to submit personal profile information.

The app may connect to remote servers to retrieve lottery-related content, app-version information, and other app data. Normal internet requests may expose technical information such as IP address, device/network information, request time, and similar connection data to the hosting provider.

2. Advertising

US Draw AI uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display ads to free users. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic data, approximate location derived from network information, and other information needed for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency capping, measurement, and, where permitted, ad personalization.

Ad behavior may vary depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, Google settings, and applicable law.

3. In-app purchases and subscriptions

VIP access is offered through Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. US Draw AI does not receive or store your full credit-card or bank-card details. Google Play may provide the app with purchase status, product identifiers, purchase tokens, and related transaction information needed to verify and provide VIP access.

4. How information is used

  • provide and maintain app functionality;
  • retrieve current app content and version information;
  • display and measure advertising;
  • process and verify VIP purchases or subscriptions;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, and security problems;
  • diagnose crashes, errors, or technical issues; and
  • comply with legal and platform requirements.

5. Sharing of information

US Draw AI does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers necessary to operate the app, including Google Play, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads, and hosting providers used to deliver app data and version information.

6. Data retention and deletion

US Draw AI does not maintain its own user-account database. Data handled by Google or other third-party providers is retained according to those providers' policies and applicable law. Local app data can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

If you have a privacy-related request, use the developer contact information shown on the Google Play Store listing for US Draw AI.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures and third-party platform services to help protect app communications and purchase-related information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

US Draw AI is not directed to children. The app is intended for adults and for entertainment and statistical research purposes. Users are responsible for complying with applicable age and legal requirements in their jurisdiction.

9. Third-party privacy policies

Third-party services used by the app operate under their own privacy policies. Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the developer contact information listed on the Google Play Store page for US Draw AI.
